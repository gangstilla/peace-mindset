# Peace Mindset: voice guide

Applies to every word on the site, including button labels, captions and prompts.

## Voice
Plain, warm and a little dry. Write the way a friend who has read a lot would talk: short sentences, everyday words, one idea at a time. Never promise results ("works", "transforms", "guaranteed"). Use "tends to", "often" and "try".

## Avoid (common AI tells)
- **Negative parallelism:** "not X, but Y", "no X, no Y, no Z", "not only... but also". State the thing directly.
- **Rule of three:** do not default to triads. Use two items or four, whichever is true.
- **Inflated importance:** no "transformative", "profound", "journey", "unlock", "holistic", "embrace", "navigate".
- **Trailing significance clauses:** do not end a sentence with "...highlighting the importance of..." or similar.
- **Stock wellness phrases:** "take a deep breath", "be kind to yourself", "you are enough", "self-care".
- **Em-dash asides, colon-then-reveal sentences, scare quotes around invented labels.**
- **Uniform rhythm:** mix short and long sentences by ear. Do not force it.
- **Autopilot transitions:** cut "Moreover", "Furthermore", "In today's fast-paced world".
- **Invented facts:** no made-up statistics or precise claims ("around minute seven") that are not sourced.

## Do
- Use a concrete detail only this subject would have ("Fill the kettle", "Notice your jaw").
- Use contractions: "it's", "you'll", "don't".
- Say what a control does: "Begin breathing", "Another prompt".
- Read each line aloud. If you would not say it, rewrite it.

## Do not over-edit
If a line is already plain and specific, leave it. A checklist can flatten the voice just as badly as the tells it removes. The patterns above are signs to check, not proof of a problem.

## Safety
This is general wellbeing content, not medical advice. Keep the footer disclaimer. Do not make claims about treating anxiety, depression or any condition.
