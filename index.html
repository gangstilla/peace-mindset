Practices

Nine things to try when your mind won't settle.

You need nothing for these except a few minutes. Pick one and give it a week before you decide.

  • Name it to tame it

    Say what you feel in one word: "anxious", "irritated", "tired". Naming an emotion tends to take some of the heat out of it.

    On a hard daySay the one word. That's it.

    30 sec

  • The long exhale

    Breathe in through your nose, then take a second short sip of air on top. Let it all out slowly through your mouth. Two or three rounds often slow things down.

    On a hard dayOne slow breath out is plenty.

    1 min

  • Five things, four, three

    Name five things you see, four you can touch, three you hear. It pulls attention out of the story in your head and into the room.

    On a hard dayName three things you can see.

    2 min

  • Schedule the worry

    Pick a fifteen-minute window each day. When a worry shows up at other times, note it down and tell it to wait until then.

    On a hard dayJot the worry in one line. It can wait for tomorrow's window.

    15 min

  • One next step

    When everything feels heavy, write down the very next physical thing you could do. Open the email. Fill the kettle. That's all.

    On a hard dayPick something tiny, like standing up.

    2 min

  • Walk without a phone

    Ten minutes outside with nothing to listen to and nothing to scroll. It's boring for a while, and then it isn't.

    On a hard dayStand at a window or the door for two minutes.

    10 min

  • Before you say yes

    Check your energy and what this would push out of your week. Then say, "I'll have a look and come back to you."

    On a hard daySay the sentence. You can work out the rest later.

    2 min

  • Rest or avoidance?

    Ask whether this break will leave you more able to choose, or just put off the thing that matters. Either answer is useful.

    On a hard dayIf you can't tell, rest first and ask again later.

    1 min

  • A fairer answer

    Write down the harsh thought. Then what supports it and what doesn't. Finish with the reply you'd give a friend.

    On a hard dayWrite only the reply you'd give a friend.

    5 min

A day in three pauses

Three short pauses, spread across the day.

Morning

What matters today?

Before the phone, pick one thing that would make today a good day, even if everything else slips.

Midday

Where is my body holding on?

Notice your jaw and your shoulders. Most people are clenching something. Let it go, then drink some water.

Evening

What can I set down?

Name one thing you are finished carrying today. Write it down or say it out loud, then leave it there.
