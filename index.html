Practices

Six things that work when your mind won't settle.

None of these need equipment or experience. Try one for a week before you judge it.

  • Name it to tame it

    Say what you feel in one word: "anxious", "irritated", "tired". Putting a label on an emotion tends to lower its intensity.

    30 sec

  • The long exhale

    Breathe in twice through the nose, a short sip on top of a full breath, then let it out slowly through the mouth. Two or three rounds settle the body fast.

    1 min

  • Five things, four, three

    Name five things you see, four you can touch, three you hear. It pulls attention out of the story in your head and into the room.

    2 min

  • Schedule the worry

    Pick a fifteen-minute window each day. When a worry shows up at other times, note it down and tell it to wait until then.

    15 min

  • One next step

    When everything feels heavy, write down only the very next physical action. Not the plan, not the outcome. Just the next move.

    2 min

  • Walk without a phone

    Ten minutes outside with nothing to listen to or scroll. Let boredom do its work. Ideas and relief often show up around minute seven.

    10 min

A day in three pauses

You don't need an hour. You need three honest minutes.

Morning

What matters today?

Before the phone, pick one thing that would make today a good day, even if everything else slips.

Midday

Where is my body holding on?

Check your jaw, shoulders and breath. Release what you find. Drink some water.

Evening

What can I set down?

Name one thing you are finished carrying today. Write it down or say it out loud, then leave it there.

Check in

What's the weather inside right now?

Write for five minutes
